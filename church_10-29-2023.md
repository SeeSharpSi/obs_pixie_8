# 1 Timothy 6:11-21
* the family of God demonstrates its love for God by its works
* We are to be contending for the truth in love
* Hebrews 11 (hall of faith) great examples
* the opposite of the "man of God" is the "man of lawlessness" (2 Thess. 2)
* What things do we flee? (v. 11)
	* the flesh
	* false gospel
	* desires of the eyes
	* pride
	* love if money
	* worldly passions
	* **all forms of ungodliness** - to do so, we must first know what is Godly 
	* _Proverbs 7:6-. . ._
	* _James 4:4_
* faith always has an object; you don't put your faith in nothing
* how do we fight the good fight of faith? (v. 12)
	* biblical hope is not like the hope of the world
	* we are to live hope-filled lives
	* knowing what our calling is
	* fight **using the word of God, the sword of the spirit** (what Jesus did in the wilderness)
	* David said Goliath's sword was great. David beat this sword with God.
* Are you taking hold if the gift of Christ? Of Him? (John 3:16) _Romans 10:6-9_
* "Before Pontius pilot, Jesus testified that he was God in the flesh, He was lord of the universe and savior of the world. Jesus testified to the truth, that all men are at enmity with God because of sin and all will be condemned to hell"
* what is the commandments? (v. 14)
	* 1 Tim. 1:5
	* the aim if our charge is love
	* love one another
	* you cannot love God if you are not loving your brothers and sisters in Christ
	* _John 15:12_
* to Him be honor and eternal dominion. **Amen** (v. 16)
	* 1 Tim. 1:15-17
	* immortal, invisible, the only God, be honor and glory forever and ever **amen** (1 Tim. 1:17)
* we are rich, here's commands to the rich (v. 17)
	* charge them not to be haughty
	* everything is God's
	* _1 Corinthians 4:7_
	* do not put your hope/expectation in earthly riches
* we are to be a people who put our hope/expectation on the riches of God's grace towards us (_Ephesians 2:5-7_)
* the rich in the church are to utilize the riches they have to do good
	* serve others, not ourselves
	* be rich in good works (_Matthew 25_)
* if you set your eye on Christ you become more like Him
* how do we guard the good news of Jesus Christ? (v. 20)
	* pray on it
	* study it
	* meditate on it
	* preach it
* if it were not for God's grace we would all swerve and choose the wide gate (_Matt. 7:13-14_)
* if we are to live Him as we should, we are to contend for His gospel. Hold fast to it, so that others will come to believe
