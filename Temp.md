RQ 7-1
The architectural design phase provides a high-level overview of the system. Large/main components are listed, as well as relationships between components. Requirements play the biggest part and drive for these overviews (for instance, what components do we need to achieve our functional/non-functional requirements?). During the detailed design phase components are broken down into more granular detail. The macro view provided in the architectural phase and the functional requirements are what drive the detailed design phase. The architecture provides a general map, while the functional requirements are addressed along the way.
