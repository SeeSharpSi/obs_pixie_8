The main goal with these three phases is to give guidance to do as much as possible to keep from getting hurt if it doesn't work out, to keep from making mistakes and having regrets. Until you are engaged to marry someone, there is a good possibility you are spending time with someone else's future spouse. The goal is to find out if this is a person that is suitable for you to make a lifelong covenant with.

Until you enter into the first circle, all guys are just friends with no intentions other than being an encouragement to them in Christ, and to be there for them in an appropriate way as a friend. If you and a guy have a mutual desire to get to know each other better, it needs to be intentional. Following these phases will help you and the guy stay where you are and not get ahead in your heart and mind. Try as hard as you can to stay within the purpose of each circle. And, keep parents and friends involved in holding you accountable to where things are to help you not get ahead. I recommend your dad be involved and even having a conversation with the young man soon about the expectations of following this model for pursuing his daughter.

Phase 1 - Could we get married? In this phase you are trying to answer the question if this person is even a possibility for you to marry. You are trying to find out if they are a real follower of Jesus. You are looking for any big red flags that would make the two of you unfit for marriage. This is a big picture phase that is intended to weed out bad fits before it goes any further. Could this even work? Would I be unequally yoked with this guy if we were to be married one day?

Phase 2 - Should we get married? Once you know that you could, that doesn't mean you should. This phase is more like "dating" but is much more intentional than how the world would define it. You spend time together to see how each other relates to others. Do they know how to truly love others? Are they humble? Would he be a good leader of a family? Do you enjoy being around each other? Do you have similar interests and goals in life? Does he have a good work ethic? Does he get on my nerves for any reason? Watching how he treats others and speaks about others is an important way to learn who he really is. As this phase progresses, it's a good idea for your family and friends to really get to know him and let them give you feedback on whether they see the two of you as a good fit for each other. In this phase, you are finding out if it would be a good idea for you to consider marriage to each other.

Phase 3 - Will we get married? This is the phase where you get some counselling and make a decision to get engaged to be married. You are agreeing to make a covenant soon, so it is a very serious time. Once you make a decision in this phase, you would start planning your wedding!

The hard part is to not allow your mind to meditate on what "could be". You will be tempted to think about him all the time and dream about life together. Try hard not to wonder ahead with your thoughts. You want your heart and mind to stay in the phase with the purpose of each phase keeping it intentional. You are guarding your heart from being deceived and possibly making bad choices along the way. My main goal with Kayla has been for her to not get her heart crushed by a guy. There is no reason for that, and it can devastate a person for a very long time.
