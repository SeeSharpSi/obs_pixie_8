# Acts 22
`Paul's defence`
* Why does Acts point out Paul speaking in Hebrews several times?
	* Paul is identifying with the people he's talking to

## 3-5
`Paul's Ethos`
* Who is Gamaliel?
	* He's from Acts 5:33-39
	* He persuaded the Pharisees to not kill the apostles
	* He was a well known and respected scholar
* What is Tarsus known for?
	* Well known for being an intellectual hub for Jewish scholars
* Why would Paul bring up his persecution of Christians?
	* To show how powerful his conversion was
* Phillippians 3:4-6
	* Demonstrates Paul's outlook on himself in relation to others. Paul doesn't boast, instead he points towards God

## 6-11
`But God...`
* Paul recounts how God reaches out to him and spiritually opened his eyes
* _Isaiah 6:9_
* God says "Saul" twice. In Hebrew culture, saying a name twice shows a personal "closeness" like God knows him closely
	* Hurting a Christian is a personal offense against God Almighty (v. 7 - "persecuting me"). Yet God was willing to forgive Paul
* How does this passage parallel the life of a believer?
	* _Phillippians 3_
