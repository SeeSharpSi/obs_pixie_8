## 1
* "Judaizers"
	* Judaism is simply the Jewish religion
	* judaizers, first century in the church preached the false gospel that followed of Christ were required to adhere to the old testament commandments, traditions, and laws
* "caught in any transgression"
	* This is translated differently, depending
* "you who are spiritual"
	* Gal. 5:16
* "restore him"
	* Assumes he's open to reproach
	* what does it mean to restore?
		* This isn't church discipline (that word is similar to disciple?)
		* 2 Cor. 2:7-8
		* be gentle, restore for the _purpose_ of love and God's glory
* "Keep watch on yourself"
	* 1 Cor. 10:12
	* Doing "good things" doesn't make sins okay
	* we must examine ourselves, not in comparison to others
		* Though having good, Christian examples (Phil. 3:17) can help us

# take-aways
* are you in a place where you need help?
	* Do you have a spiritual struggle where you could use some restoring, care, sharing of the burden.
	* is there a believer around you who can help?
	* let's let "this rubber hit the road"
* is there someone around you that you might help? Or that needs help?
	* Maybe you identify that but don't know how to help? Or not used to helping spiritually?
	* You can come to Pastor Mike or "myself" (Pastor Rob)
* what is the family of God about?
	* Fellowship
	* discipleship
	* accountability
	* burden sharing
	* Many, many more...
	* ultimately to glorify God together
* for each will have to bear his own load
	* no-one else can obey the Lord for you. 
	* No-one else can submit to Jesus for you. 
	* No-one else can grow for you. Etc.
