# Luke 2
## "paradoxes"
* The appearance to a Shepard
	* They were over flocks of sheep
	* Because of job, could not participate in many Jewish lifestyle things
	* Lowliest of the low
* the glory of the Lord around the Shepherds
	* God's glory shone around/towards people only through set "things" like the tabernacle
	* still, God's glory shone around the Shepards (v. 9)
* that glory pointed towards a manger
	* Most likely a feeding trough
* peace was obtained by God alone
	* God's movement of peace is from heaven to earth. But it requires a response from those so favored that it moves from earth so that it moves back from earth to heaven.
* v. 15
	* "Go and see Jesus"
* v. 17
	* The Shepards reported what they'd seen and all were amazed
