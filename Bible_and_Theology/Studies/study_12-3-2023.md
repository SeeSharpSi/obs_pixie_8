# Acts 22
`Paul's defence`
* Why does Acts point out Paul speaking in Hebrews several times?
	* Paul is identifying with the people he's talking to
* This is Paul's testimony of his conversion. Whereas Acts 9 is Luke's account of it

## 3-5
`Paul's Ethos`
* Who is Gamaliel?
	* He's from Acts 5:33-39
	* He persuaded the Pharisees to not kill the apostles
	* He was a well known and respected scholar
* What is Tarsus known for?
	* Well known for being an intellectual hub for Jewish scholars
* Why would Paul bring up his persecution of Christians?
	* To show how powerful his conversion was
* Phillippians 3:4-6
	* Demonstrates Paul's outlook on himself in relation to others. Paul doesn't boast, instead he points towards God

## 6-11
`But God...`
* Paul recounts how God reaches out to him and spiritually opened his eyes
* _Isaiah 6:9_
* God says "Saul" twice. In Hebrew culture, saying a name twice shows a personal "closeness" like God knows him closely
	* Hurting a Christian is a personal offense against God Almighty (v. 7 - "persecuting me"). Yet God was willing to forgive Paul
* How does this passage parallel the life of a believer?
	* _Phillippians 3_

## 12-16
`God's direct mission for Paul`
* Ananias:
	* Was a renown Jewish scholar
	* Acts 9
	* Ananias was given a vision of Paul's conversion
	* Ananias knew of Paul's "deeds"
* v. 14 - it's "cool" what Ananias said to Paul; Steven said something similar when he was martyred
* it's cool seeing God's will working. Acts 22:14, Acts 9:15-16
	* ==God has a purpose for ***all*** of it.==

## 17-21
* v. 20-21 are a stark contrast

## 22-30
* The crowd hears "gentiles (v. 21)" and is not happy. Still, it's amazing how the crowd even allowed Paul to speak this long
	* May be angry because it seems like God has chosen others for Himself, instead of the Jews exclusively
	* "Gentile" may have also been a dirty word to the Jews, as their persecutors (throughout time) were gentiles
* Paul gets out of a flogging. The Roman commander still wanted to know the Jews' dispute against Paul, ending the chapter with "Then he brought Paul and had him stand before them."
