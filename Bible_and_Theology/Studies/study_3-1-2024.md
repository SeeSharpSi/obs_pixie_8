#bible #1-corinthians

# 1 Corinthians 4

## v 1-5
* We are to be stewards
	* ==Steward==: being a keeper of something you do not own 
* Do not judge others, for God is the ultimate judge 
	* Reprimand brothers in Christ. But the judgement found here is bad
	* what is "the time?" (v. 5)
* v. 4 what if I was/am aware of something against myself; what would that look like? 
	* Conviction
	* repentance 
	* change, going forwards
* v. 5 "judgement" is a positive thing 
	* ". . . Then each one will receive his commendation from God"
	* this plays out through the rest of the chapter
		* v. 6 ". . . puffed up"
		* v. 8 ". . . become kings" 
		* v. 18 ". . . some are arrogant"

## v 6-7
* Don't conclude things outside of scripture, and don't be boastful of things found within scripture 
	* Finding something within scripture is not a divine revelation specific to yourself (though followers do have the holy Spirit / ears to hear) 
	* finding something outside of scripture is not... scriptural 
	* "What do you have that you did not receive" - all we have is from God 

## v 8-13
* This is ==admonishment== 
	* a firm warning or reprimand 
	* he does this in love (v. 14)

> [!bible]+ [1 Corinthians 1:27-29 - ESV](https://bolls.life/ESV/46/1/)
> 27. But God chose what is foolish in the world to shame the wise; God chose what is weak in the world to shame the strong;
> 28. God chose what is low and despised in the world, even things that are not, to bring to nothing things that are,
> 29. so that no human being  might boast in the presence of God.

## v 14-21 
* he writes things things (v. 8-13) as a loving ==admonishment== to the church in Corinth
* v. 18 - those arrogant are from the church in Corinth, as if Paul was not to come and see what they were doing 
	* This kick-starts chapter 5 
	* Paul sends Timothy to be a living example to them, and to probably report back 
	* Paul will visit them to see their spiritual power for themselves 
* v. 15 they need an example to follow, not a partial discipleship 
* v. 15-16 is _not_ saying
	* Be part of the church of Paul (1 Cor. 3:4) 
* v. 15-16 _are_ saying 
	* 1 Cor. 3:5-9
	* Guide is 
		* Apollos "watering" ..?
		* someone who says what to do 
	* Paul isn't just telling them stuff to do, he's living it out (hence "father") 
	* Paul is sending Timothy to be a living example to them 
	* Philippians 3:17 
	* Ephesians 4:4-6
		* Paul's not trying to be God 
* v. 21 is a direct transition into chapter 5 
	* "It is actually reported that..."
