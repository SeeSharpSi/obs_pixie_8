# Psalm 78
* My wife and I have very distinct testimonies: 
	* I was raised apart from faith 
	* My wife was raised in the faith 
* It can be felt that my type of testimony is more exciting. This thinking is upside-down 
	* Which of these types of testimonies would I prefer my own children to have? (rhetorical) 
* Let's look at some hard truths: 
	* My wife has three siblings. They are all serving the Lord 
	* I have several siblings. They are not. 
* Proverbs 22:6 
	* This isn't a promise. It's a Proverb (a wise saying based upon a godly principle) 
	* Raising children in God is the norm throughout the Bible. "Hoping" they stumble into faith is bad. 
* Clarity for this message: 
	1. None of us are perfect parents 
	2. We love on and pray on our family members even if they're lost 
* We have several generations 
	* Several years ago the world decided one of these generations was the absolute worst (millennials) lol 
	* I heard a lot of talk about this generation being lost 
	* There was almost no talk of, if an entire generation was lost, whose fault was it? 
	* There is a cultural stream in the world. In a sense, the church should be the counter cultural stream in that it doesn't change its views 
* This Psalm is attributed "A maskil of Asaph" 
	* 13 Psalms are a maskil (not only to Asaph) 
	* Maskil is a musical meaning 
	* but it can also relate to the function of the Psalm / its place in worship 
	* a form of maskil is found in Amos 5 where it translated as prudent or insightful 
* Who is Asaph? 
	* Asaph was the chief of the ministers in Chronicles who David entrusted the worship of the Ark 
	* He was described as a skilled musician 
* v. 1-8 intro and purpose
* v. 9-end telling of lesson by way of history 
* We must bring up the next generation to know the Lord
