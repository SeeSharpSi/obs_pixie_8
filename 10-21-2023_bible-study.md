# preface
* John records Pilot's question: "are you, [Jesus], the king of the Jews?" Jesus' response gives insight towards our Christian outlook on the world and heaven.
* Hebrews talks of the relation between the earthly tabernacle and heaven itself (in regards to Jesus's sacrifice). It also speaks on Jesus's role as our High Priest / mediator.

# questions
* it's easy to get caught up in the world and in life. As Christians, how does our outlook on life look in regards to earth, heaven, etc.?
* How can this help us in life?
* How will this help us grow closer to Jesus?
