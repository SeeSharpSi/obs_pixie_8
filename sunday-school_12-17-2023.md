# Ephesians 2
* v. 2 and you were dead
	* v. 4-6 when we were dead
	* ch. 1:20
* Spiritually dead is the default position of man
	* Unable to do what God designed us for
	* we stand condemned
	* it's used in a metaphorical sense. "The store was dead last Sunday" because there were few customers
* v. 9
	* It's a gift in its entirety; us having the chance to accept Christ as Savior and therefore be saved from sin is a gift
	* grace is feminine 
	* faith is feminine
	* saved is masculine 
	* "this" is neutered
	* gift doesn't refer to one specifically
