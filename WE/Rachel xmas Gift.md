The air stings, pricks against each person's skin
As winter's chill comes seeping in

The remainder of this month's subtle, shrinking light 
Overshadowed by fake seasonal blithe 

A yell, then a whisper, now a lost voice
Cheer was killed. Not by chance, by choice

We once opposed evil; it was the human propensity
But our pride made good the enemy

For what purpose? To oppose peace, joy, or fun?
To enjoy moonlight, we ended the sun 

Now all is dark, except a single star in the sky
Its movements odd, but you don't know why

With reluctance, and no purpose for the season
You watch the star for some reason

As the star moves around, it rests above a stable 
It wants to gesture, but isn't able 

So you look in the shack, to see what there may be
It's shepherds worshiping a baby

Now looking up, you see a cross on a slope 
For some reason you sense hope 

But the cross has a path from its foot to the hay
And you stare at the child as He lay

The child is the purpose, and the cross the child's
That God and Man may be reconciled

You start to weep, understanding this baby's future 
That He will be good, but suffer

Yet as you struggle to see through tears of pain 
All disappears and one scene remains

An empty, white tomb with its stone rolled away 
And a man, shining like the day 

The man has a path; starting at His feet and ending at yours
Will you take the path given by the savior? 









The Creative One sparked this globe's beginning
There was joy, peace, and singing 

It was the perfect world, but we didn't care a fraction 
We threw it away in one action

Fake gods - idol worship - made humanity idle 
Blocks of wood to tease us, our devil's bridle

Every conscious now grasps in a foggy milieu
Each wisp a wrongdoing, sticky like glue

You hear screams and weeping; humans buried alive
Gasping, their spirits try to survive

Shovels cover them with all that they earn
Wages that kill, bury, and burn

For look, the day is coming, burning like a furnace
Its fuel will be humanity's corpus

But...

A glimmering star wakes up, with a big yawn 
To guide humanity before the dawn

The sky is dark blue, and clear with white specks
Like someone's 



The pines seem heavy in the breeze
And angels sing of holy things
