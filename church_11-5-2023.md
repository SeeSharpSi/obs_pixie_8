# Malachi 1
* Written around 425 B.C.
* temple rebuilt
* remnant of Jews found in promise land; Malachi writes to these Jews
* Malachi translates to "my messenger"
* Malachi reminds us that "it is only in God's provision of his son, our lord Jesus Christ, that all of God's promises and new life come"
* Narnia: "is the Lion safe?" "No, but he's good"
* Oracle can be translated as "burden"
* Israel saw God's word as a burden. Do we make the same mistake?
* when we refuse to pray, or don't read the word, or don't forgive, we're not detracting from God. **Who's that hurting?**
* are we despising Christ?
* sick sacrifices are signs of sick people (or priests)
* fear, love, and honor of God are all linked. They cannot be divided
* remember how God has loved you in and through Jesus Christ
* fear sin, fear God. Sin separates us from God, and God will not tolerate our continuing in sin
* honor the Lord with your best, always. In everything that you do. Every moment that you're living is an act of worship
* **holiness is the end game**; we are being conformed into the likeness of Christ
* suffering and holiness go hand-in-hand for a Christian
* through all of our failings remember God's promise: the Lord will not leave you, nor will He forsake you (bring to completion, yoke easy burden light)
