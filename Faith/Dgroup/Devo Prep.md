# Pray

# Short Context 
"This passage starts with Jesus returning to what's likely Peter's home in Capernaum — and it's the 1st of 5 controversies in Mark that would cause the Pharisees to plot Jesus's death."

# Read
Mk. 2:1-12 

# More Context 
You have several people in this story: the scribes (Jewish scholars), Jesus, the paralyzed man and his friends, and the crowds.

# Questions 
1. What did the paralytic come to accomplish? And what did Jesus do for him first?
2. Why is the faith of the paralytic and his friends commended?
3. Why were the scribes upset? What was Jesus trying to show them?
	1. Could field answers and then "the son of man. . . authority to forgive sins" Daniel 7:13-14
	2. "So he's not just saying He can forgive sins, He's saying He's the son of man" 
4. Do you think it's easier to say "your sins are forgiven", or to say "get up and walk"? 

# Pray Again
Jude 24-25
To him who is able to keep us from stumbling and to present us blameless before the presence of his glory with great joy, to the only God, our Savior, through Jesus Christ our Lord, be glory, majesty, dominion, and authority, before all time and now and forever. Amen.

# Where to Land it
1. God asks for us to have faith 
2. Reminds us that our greatest need is reconciliation with God, which is a result of forgiveness for our sins
