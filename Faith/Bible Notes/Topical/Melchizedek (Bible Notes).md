# General Themes 
* Melchizedek is mentioned in three sections of scripture (Genesis 14, Psalm 110, Hebrews 5-7) 
* Keep three things in mind: 
	1. The Bible is a collection of books 
	2. God is present in the authorship of each book (2 Tim. 3:16) 
	3. The events of each section we'll cover happened ~1k years apart 
		* Genesis 14 - 2,000 B.C. 
		* Psalm 110  - 1,000 B.C. 
		* Hebrews    - 60 A.D. 

# OT Priests 
* Before Aaron (Moses's brother), a priest was a tribal patriarch 
* Eventually, God made Aaron a dedicated High Priest (Exodus 28:1) 
* After this, priests had to be a descendent of Aaron, who was a descendent of Levi (hence the term "The Levitical Priesthood") 
* The most important role of a High Priest was to enter The Most Holy Place to offer blood for the atonement of Israel's sins, **including his own** 
* Priests were mortal, meaning their duty as priest would eventually end
