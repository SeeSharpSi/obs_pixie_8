# Bookshelves for Small Apartments

Wall-friendly bookshelves ideal for small apartments, ranging from budget-friendly to investment pieces.

## Wall-Mounted & Floating Options

- **String Shelving System (Design Within Reach)** – Scandinavian wall-mounted system with endless customization (wood or metal shelves, cabinets, cubbies). Minimalist, expands to fit your wall. Great for small spaces since it uses zero floor space. ~$356+
- **Shelf Expression Floating Bookshelves** – Solid hardwood, 1.8" thick, mounted with Hovr brackets rated at **150 lbs per stud**. Lifetime guarantee against warping/sagging. Sizes 12"–72". Handmade in Charlotte, NC. If you want shelves that truly last decades, these are built for it.
- **IKEA LACK Wall Shelves** – Budget option ($10–25 each). Lightweight, clean look, but only holds ~22 lbs per shelf. Good for a small collection of paperbacks.

## Leaning / Ladder Bookshelves

- **Article Krossa Shelving Unit** – ~$800. Tapered Scandi design in white oak or walnut. Reinforced joinery. Very sturdy. Wall-mountable for safety. Best if you have a bit more room.
- **Nathan James Theo Ladder Bookshelf (Set of 2)** – ~$220. Black metal frame + engineered wood shelves, **50 lbs per shelf**. Lifetime guarantee. Wall-mountable, modular — pair multiple units for a built-in look.
- **Giantex Ladder Bookshelf with Cabinet** – ~$150-200. Features rattan-cabinet doors, 4-point wall anchoring, and a space-saving vertical design (72.5" tall).

## Narrow / Slim Options

- **HOOBRO 70.9" Narrow Bookcase** – Only 9.4" wide! Perfect for tight gaps, corners, behind doors. Includes cabinet + drawer + open shelves. Anti-tip wall anchor included. Budget-friendly.
- **Breur & Bono Schlagseite** – Ultra-slim wall-mounted grid (only 11cm deep). Angled shelves hold books without bookends. Birch multiplex + stainless steel. Premium European design (~€755).

## Top Picks for Durability

1. **Shelf Expression** – Best long-term investment, solid hardwood, lifetime guarantee
2. **String System** – Best modular wall-mounted option
3. **Nathan James Theo** – Best value with lifetime guarantee
