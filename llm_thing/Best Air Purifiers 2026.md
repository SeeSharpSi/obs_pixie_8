## What to Look for When Buying an Air Purifier

### 1. Filter Type
- **True HEPA** is the most important: captures **99.97%** of particles ≥0.3 microns (dust, pollen, smoke, bacteria). Look for "True HEPA" — not "HEPA-like" or "HEPA-type."
- **Activated Carbon** (for odors, VOCs, gases): if you need strong odor/VOC removal, look for **pelleted or granular** carbon (much more effective than thin carbon sheets).
- **Washable Pre-filter**: catches large particles (pet hair, lint) and extends main filter life.

### 2. Room Size Coverage & CADR
- **CADR** (Clean Air Delivery Rate): measures cubic feet/minute of cleaned air on high speed. Higher = faster cleaning.
- Check the CADR, especially for your target pollutant (separate CADR ratings exist for smoke/dust/pollen).
- Rule of thumb: **CADR should be ≥ 2/3 of your room's area (sq ft)** — e.g., 300 sq ft room → CADR ~200+.
- Look for **AHAM Verifide** certification for trusted CADR numbers.

### 3. Air Changes Per Hour (ACH)
- Aim for **4–5 ACH** for solid filtration. Multiply room's cubic feet × ACH, then divide by 60 for required CFM.

### 4. Noise Level
- Critical for bedrooms/living areas — look for **≤45 dBA** at lower speeds.
- Check specs for noise range at each fan speed.

### 5. Smart & Convenience Features
- **Air quality sensors** that auto-adjust fan speed.
- **Display-off mode** (let it keep running while you sleep).
- **Filter replacement indicator light**.
- Smart/app controls for scheduling and remote operation.

### 6. Operating & Maintenance Costs
- Check **annual filter replacement cost** (varies from $20 to $200+).
- **Energy Star certified** units use less electricity (some use <5W on low).
- Some models have washable filters (lower cost, but check cleaning effort).

### 7. Certifications to Look For
- **AHAM Verifide**: verified CADR.
- **Energy Star**: energy efficiency.
- **CARB certified** (California Air Resources Board): meets ozone safety standards.

### 8. Watch Out For
- **Ozone generators**: avoid — can harm lungs; ionizers should be optional/disablable.
- **UV-C light**: generally secondary to HEPA; UV-C needs sufficient "dwell time" to inactivate microbes.
- Overstated room coverage claims.
