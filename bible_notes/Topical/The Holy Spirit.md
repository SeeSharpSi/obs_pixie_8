# What the Holy Spirit Does 

## For _The Believer_ 
* New believers receive the Holy Spirit 
	* Galatians 3:26, 4:6
	* Romans 8:15-16
* Paul uses the term [_plerophoria_](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/g4136/rsv/mgnt/0-1/) to indicate that a believer has knowledge of the truth as a result of the Spirit's work
	* [Colossians 2:2](https://www.blueletterbible.org/tools/interlinear/mgnt/col/2/2/); [1 Thessalonians 1:5](https://www.blueletterbible.org/tools/interlinear/mgnt/1th/1/5/); cf. Romans 4:21, 14:5; Colossians 4:12
* The apostle John also makes it clear that the Holy Spirit gives believers conviction of the truth of Christianity 
	* 1 John 2:20, 27
	* John 14:26
* 1 John 4:1-3 is not talking of the Holy Spirit itself, but of testing others that say their claim comes from the Holy Spirit
	* John speaks of this earlier in 1 John 2:18-19
	* The inner witness of the Holy Spirit isn't to be tested. Rather, the claims that others attribute to the Holy Spirit should be tested since they're external
* The Holy Spirit gives the believer certainty that Jesus lives with him 
	* John 14:16-17, 20
* 1 John 5:6-10
	* "The water" is probably baptism, "the blood" Christ's crucifixion 
	* Christ's baptism and crucifixion are the bookends to His Earthly ministry

## For _The Unbeliever_ 
* John 16:7-11
	* God has a special ministry for the Holy Spirit for the unbeliever
	* Non-believers are actively rejecting the Holy Spirit's pull
