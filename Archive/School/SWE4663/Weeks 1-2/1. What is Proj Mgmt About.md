**What is software?**

* All, part, or only one of the following:

	* Executable Code 
	* Requirements Specifications 
	* Design Documents

**Software Engineering**
* Set of specific _skills and knowledge_ applied to the development and service of software "artifacts" 
* _Process and activities_ applied to the development and service of software artifacts 
* _Characterizing and modeling_ software and processes; then relating them to skills, knowledge, activities, etc. 

**What is Management?**
* General Management
* Applying the various "management" techniques to achieve the end goal (_Assumes that there are clearly state project goals!_) 
* Providing and _environment_ that creates the _end results_ in a _most efficient_ manner 
* Applying the needed _resources_ to achieve the _desired results_ (which may include _efficiency_ and other attributes) 
* Set of _processes_ that develops an environment where people produces the desires _end results_ in an efficient manner 

**Management** 
* Art or Science? 
	* Both 

**Management: Art or Science?** 
* Management is: 
	* _Science_: it is based on the known management "principles" and "techniques" 
		* Principles: are <u>descriptive</u> and possibly <u>predictive</u> 
		* Techniques: are mostly <u>prescriptive</u> 
	* _Art_: it is "applying" these principles and techniques 
		* Dependent on different situations, which may be infinite 
		* Dependent on the managers own styles and preference

**Managers == Leaders ?**
* Difference between Managers and Leaders? 
	* Leaders _create_ and _commit_ to a vision/direction 
	* Managers _plan_, _organize_, and _direct_ the activities towards achieving the vision and direction 
Leaders/Managers _inspire_ others to follow the direction of the vision/direction
