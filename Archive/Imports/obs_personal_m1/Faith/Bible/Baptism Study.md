* By summoning Jews to a baptism of repentance, John the Baptist was implicitly stating that the Jewish lineage and the sign of circumcision were insufficient to prepare them for the coming Messiah and to save them from the "coming wrath"
* John's baptism was not for ritual impurity but for moral and spiritual repentance. It was an individual act of confession and turning back to God, severing the automatic link between ethnic heritage and right standing with God 
* The Christian community is defined not by birth, but by faith 
* Acts 2:38 - be baptized "for the forgiveness of sins" - if someone is fighting "for America," does that mean America does or doesn't already exist? 
* The common theme in Acts is that belief leads to salvation
