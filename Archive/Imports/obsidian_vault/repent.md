# post-modernism and the missing word
A current trend in philosophy is the revival of agnosticism and, more directly, christianity. The idea that we can reason for a God and that the most reasonable God is that of the Bible has taken certain sections of academia by storm. Unfortunately, this trend has caused a drifting of an essential aspect of the gospel: repentance.

# Mark 1:4
> [!bible]+ [Mark 1:4 - ESV](https://bolls.life/ESV/41/1/)
> 4. John appeared, baptizing in the wilderness and proclaiming a baptism of repentance for the forgiveness of sins.

John the Baptist clearly states a baptism of repentance. Belief in God is not salvation; belief with repentance and an understanding of God as our Lord is.

Sin is scary. The idea of us, ourselves, me, myself, doing something damnable is scary. Self empowerment is not scary. The idea of myself finding the truth of God and who He is feels fulfilling.
These two truths often cause us to forget about repentance.

An idea that has plagued the south is the understanding that most Americans have heard of Christ. But most Americans do not understand that repentance away from sin is critical to the gospel. Sin cannot enter the presence of a holy god.
