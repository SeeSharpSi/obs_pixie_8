# Focus on God 

> [!bible]+ [Psalm 27:4 - ESV](https://bolls.life/ESV/19/27/)
> 4. One thing have I asked of the LORD, that will I seek after: that I may dwell in the house of the LORD all the days of my life, to gaze upon the beauty of the LORD and to inquire  in his temple.

Josie, you are awesome and I am so blessed to have such a loving, smart, funny, kind sister. My prayer and exhortation for you is to make God the center of your life. Love Him dearly, just as He loved you on the cross. As R.C. Sproul said: "Why do bad things happen to good people? That only happened once, and He volunteered." God's love goes beyond a simple kindness or feeling and He shows it time and time again. 

I know you love God. Keep on loving and focusing on Him. It can be hard: good times can cause forgetfulness, bad times can cause anger. But He's always there, and always the same. God told Israel to take care to remember Him. They did for a time, but they eventually forgot. They eventually moved their focus. 
Above is Psalm 27:4, one of my favorite Bible verses. It is a reminder of David's want for the presence, wisdom, and beauty of God. If you ever feel off-kilter look to God. He loves you. He's beautiful. He's wise. 

"Now to ==Him who is able to keep you from stumbling== and to present you blameless before the presence of his glory with great joy, to the only God, our Savior, through Jesus Christ our Lord, be glory, majesty, dominion, and authority, before all time and now and forever. Amen." (Jude 1:24-25)

Love ya Josie!
