Do you worship God because of Him, because of what He's done for you, because of His greatness, His goodness, His justice, because He's infinite? Or do you worship God because He's called God..? But you aren't sure what that means. 
Do a mental exercise with me; humor me:
You live in the year 50 A.D.
Your parents are Jewish, and you've been raised to know the Old Testament very well; just like your friends. 
You know many things, including Isaiah 40, verse 3:
> [!bible]+ [Isaiah 40:3 - LSB](https://bolls.life/LSB/23/40/)
> 3. A voice is calling,<br/>“Prepare the way for Yahweh in the wilderness;<br/>Make smooth in the desert a highway for our God.<br/>

One day, while you sit in the town square, a man named Peter starts speaking. Turn to your passage:
Mark 1:1-4, 7 

> [!bible]+ [John 1:29 - LSB](https://bolls.life/LSB/43/1/)
> 29. On the next day, he saw Jesus coming to him and said, “Behold, the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world!
