## role of the Holy Spirit 
* 43.5 Plantinga and Dodwell seem correct in that, fundamentally, the way we know Christianity is true is by the self-authenticating witness of God's Holy Spirit 
	* The experience of the Holy Spirit is veridical and unmistakable (though not necessarily irresistible or indubitable) 
* How does the Holy Spirit affect believers vs unbelievers? 

### _The Believer_ 
* New believers receive the Holy Spirit 
	* Gal. 3:26, 4:6 
	* Romans 8:15-16 
* Paul uses the term _plerophoria_ to indicate that a believer has knowledge of the truth as a result of the Spirit's work 
	* Col. 2:2; 1 These. 1:5; cf. Rom. 4:21; 14:5; Col. 4:12 
* The apostle John also makes it clear that the Holy Spirit gives believers conviction of the truth of Christianity 
	* 1 John 2:20, 27 
	* John 14:26 
* 1 John 4:1-3 is not talking of the Holy Spirit itself, but of testing others that say their claim comes from the Holy Spirit 
	* John speaks of this earlier in 1 John 2:18-19 
* The inner witness of the Holy Spirit isn't to be tested. Rather, the claims that others attribute to the Holy Spirit should be tested since they're external 
* The Holy Spirit gives the believer certainty that Jesus lives with him 
	* John 14:16-17, 20
